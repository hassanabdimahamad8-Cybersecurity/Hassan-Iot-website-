Academic Programs

The Institute offers undergraduate programs focused on engineering and computing. Below are key programs currently associated with the Institute of Technology:

Civil Engineering

Design, construction, and maintenance of infrastructure including roads, buildings, and water systems suited to regional conditions.

Water Resource and Irrigation Engineering

Focus on water management, irrigation systems, and solutions for arid and semi-arid environments.

Electrical & Computer Engineering

Power systems, electronics, computer hardware/software integration, and related technologies.

Software Engineering

Systematic design, development, testing, and maintenance of software systems.

Computer Science

Core computing theory, algorithms, programming, and computational problem-solving.

Information Technology

Practical IT systems, networks, applications, and support for organizational needs.

Construction Technology and Management

Construction processes, project management, and technology application in building works.

Survey Engineering

Land surveying, mapping, and geospatial techniques essential for planning and development.

Program availability and exact curricula follow university and Ministry of Education guidelines. Contact the admissions office for the latest list and prerequisites.
