About the Institute of Technology

The Jigjiga Institute of Technology (JiT / JIoT) is a specialized branch of Jigjiga University, established to meet regional and national demand for skilled professionals in engineering, technology, and applied sciences.

History

Jigjiga University was founded in 2007 as one of Ethiopia’s second-generation public universities. The Institute of Technology developed as a key academic unit focused on technical education suited to the needs of the Somali Region and the wider country, with emphasis on infrastructure, water resources, computing, and innovation.

Vision

To be a leading institute that produces competent, innovative, and socially responsible graduates who drive sustainable technological and infrastructural development.

Mission

To provide quality education, practical training, and research that addresses the unique challenges of arid and semi-arid regions—particularly in civil works, water management, electrical systems, software, and information technology—while serving students, faculty, and the broader community.

Educational Philosophy

We combine rigorous academic coursework with practical skills, community-oriented projects, and digital literacy. Graduates are prepared to contribute to regional development, national growth, and lifelong learning.
