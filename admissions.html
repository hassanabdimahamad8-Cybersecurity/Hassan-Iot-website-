Admissions

Admission to programs at the Institute of Technology follows the criteria set by the Ministry of Education and Jigjiga University for regular, extension, evening, and summer modalities.

General Requirements

  • Successful completion of Ethiopian Secondary School Leaving Certificate Examination (or equivalent) with the required grades for the chosen program.
  • Meeting the university’s cut-off points and any program-specific prerequisites (especially for engineering fields).
  • Submission of required documents (transcripts, identification, application form) within announced deadlines.
  • For continuing/distance programs: additional criteria as published by the Registrar.

Important Notes

Exact deadlines, entrance requirements, and available seats are announced by the University Registrar each academic year. Prospective students should check official university channels or contact the Registrar’s Office.
